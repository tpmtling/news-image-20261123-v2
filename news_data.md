- Lead: COP31 collapsed in Antalya on Sunday after more than two weeks
  of talks: countries failed to agree on an overarching deal, the
  first breakdown of UN climate talks since Copenhagen in 2009 and the
  longest COP in history. The EU, Panama, the Marshall Islands and
  Norway rejected the "Antalya Pact" because it never mentioned fossil
  fuels; the draft also dropped a $120bn adaptation-finance target.
  UN chief Antonio Guterres said he was deeply disappointed.

  (AFP / Al Jazeera, 22 November 2026)
